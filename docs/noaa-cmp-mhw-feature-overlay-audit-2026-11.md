# NOAA CMP feature-level MHW shoreline overlay review (November 2026)

## Purpose

This research-only review compares the actual NOAA National Geodetic Survey Coastal Mapping Program (CMP) Natural Mean High Water (MHW) feature vectors for FL1808A-TB-N and FL1808C-TB-N against two separate spatial contexts:

1. NOAA ENC charted named-water polygons named **Biscayne Bay** (37 chart features).
2. FDEP Biscayne Bay Aquatic Preserve, OBJECTID 43.

The separate FDEP Biscayne Bay-Cape Florida to Monroe County Line preserve (OBJECTID 25) is also checked and remains separate. Measurements use NAD83 / UTM Zone 17N (EPSG:26917). Source geometries remain WGS84 in the repository.

The four mutually exclusive partition classes account for the full source line length in each project. They are not quality scores. In particular, linework outside both polygons is not automatically missing, invalid, or misplaced shoreline.

## Feature-level findings

The audit now retains a record for every MHW source feature, including its NOAA feature ID, source date, reported horizontal accuracy, total length, length inside each comparison polygon, mutually exclusive partition lengths, and up to three intersected NOAA named-water context polygons with chart-cell identifiers.

This is intended to make review actionable: a reviewer can identify which exact source features contribute to a polygon overlap, instead of relying only on project-wide percentages. Named-water hits are contextual chart references, not proof of a channel, habitat, water exchange, or navigability.

## Source geometry and seam interpretation

- FL1808A: 58 features, source date 2018-07-26, reported horizontal accuracy 2.3 m.
- FL1808C: 70 features; 65 features are dated 2018-07-26 with 2.3 m accuracy and five are dated 2019-04-12/13 with 1.3 m accuracy.
- The closest FL1808A/FL1808C MHW linework pair is 17,690.77 m apart (feature IDs 2077140 and 1983664). These are distinct extracted project coverage areas, not adjacent shoreline ends. This is not an alongshore gap and must not be bridged.
- The earlier FL1603A/FL1808A contact remains a separate candidate local seam for manual review (0.00 m minimum line distance; 0.18 m endpoint distance). The two measurements refer to different closest feature pairs and are not an accepted conflation.
- FL2317-CS-N contains no Natural.Mean High Water features. Its port structures must not be substituted for natural shoreline or a Government Cut channel centerline.

## Guardrails

- NOAA ENC charted named-water polygons are chart context, not a complete ecological or statutory Bay boundary.
- FDEP preserve polygons are approximate management GIS boundaries, not legal survey or enforcement geometry.
- MHW linework is not bathymetry, a channel centerline, or evidence of tidal exchange, fish passage, habitat, or navigation safety.
- MHW and MHHW remain separate products.
- No snapping, dissolving across source projects, gap bridging, or synthetic inlet connections are performed.
- Government Cut, Norris Cut, Bear Cut, and other inlet connections remain unresolved until supported by appropriate hydrographic evidence.
- No production scoring, species files, API contracts, or production spatial layers are changed.
